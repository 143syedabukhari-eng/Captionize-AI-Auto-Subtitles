Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2024

Welcome to CapScribe. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our AI-powered captioning services, we collect the following information:

  • Personal Data: We collect your email address and a unique User ID when you sign in via Firebase Authentication.
  • Video Data: We process the videos you select for captioning. These videos are uploaded to our secure storage partner for processing.
  • App Activity: We track usage metrics (such as project counts) to manage free trial limits and system performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To generate intelligent subtitles and captions for your videos.
  • To maintain your personal project library across devices.
  • To enforce usage limits (e.g., the 17-project global trial and 10MB upload limits).
  • To provide technical support and respond to your requests.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share specific data with trusted third-party providers to enable core app features. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google): For user authentication and project database management.
  • Cloudinary: For secure video hosting and processing.
  • AssemblyAI / Deepgram: For converting video audio into text.
  • Google Gemini: For AI text refinement and language translation.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We believe you should have control over your data:

  • User Control: You can delete any video project from your library at any time within the app. Deleting a project removes the metadata from Firestore and the video from Cloudinary.
  • Account Deletion: You may request full account deletion by contacting us at the email provided below.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • User-provided API keys (BYOK mode) are stored locally on your device using EncryptedSharedPreferences.

6. Children's Privacy

CapScribe does not knowingly collect or solicit information from anyone under the age of 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under age 13, we will delete that information as quickly as possible.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
